Rating Scale Guide

1 - Novice: No practical experience; fundamental knowledge only.
2 - Beginner: Limited practical experience; requires close supervision.
3 - Competent: Independent execution of standard tasks; moderate experience.
4 - Proficient: Deep understanding; handles complex scenarios independently.
5 - Expert: Recognized authority; mentors others; drives innovation.
